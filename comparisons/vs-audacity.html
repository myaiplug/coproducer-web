CoProducer vs Audacity

Updated August 2026 · Editor vs score

Audacity is a free DAW-adjacent editor: cut, fade, amplify, export. It will not tell you that Apple Sound Check wants −16 LUFS or that your true peak will clip an AAC encode. CoProducer will not replace a timeline editor. Together they cover “change the file” and “prove the file.”
