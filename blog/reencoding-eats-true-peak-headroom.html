Re-encoding eats true-peak headroom

NoDAW Labs · Lacquer Vol. 02

Every lossy encode can invent new inter-sample peaks. Leaving only 0.1 dB of true-peak margin is how a “clean” WAV becomes a clipped AAC. Keep ≤ −1.0 dBTP on the file you hand to the encoder.

Measure the file you actually upload

If you normalize, dither, then encode, measure after the last PCM step you control. Do not assume the DAW meter equals the distributor encode.

BeatGoHard’s lesson still holds: clips can be zero while true peak fails.

A practical margin

−1.0 dBTP is the common music-store ceiling. Amazon often wants −2.0. If you deliver one master to many stores, the stricter ceiling wins.

Re-measure after any “final final” bounce.

Revisit true peak and A/B BeatGoHard on the homepage.
Does MP3 need more margin than AAC?
Treat both as hungry. Prefer a real true-peak meter over folklore.
Can ScrewAI slowdown skip this?
No. Re-measure screwed renders if they must ship clean.
