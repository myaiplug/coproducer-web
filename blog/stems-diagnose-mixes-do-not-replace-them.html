Measure a mix

Stems · 21 August 2026 · 7 min

Stems diagnose mixes — they do not replace them

NoDAW Labs · Lacquer Vol. 02

Stem separation isolates parts so you can hear collisions. It does not invent a better arrangement. Use StemSplit to diagnose; use the mix session to fix.

Solo is a flashlight

Solo vocals against bass to hear masking. Solo drums to hear whether clip energy is transient or tone. Then return to the full mix and decide.

The homepage stem solo exists as proof, not as a mastering chain.

Local separation

NoDAW StemSplit keeps separation on your machine. That matches the same privacy story as CoProducer analysis: measurement without surrendering the unreleased file.

Hear stems on this site, split your own tracks in the app.

Can I master from stems only?
You can try. Most records still need the shared bus decisions of a real mix.
Do stems fix true peak?
No. They help you find which element is eating the ceiling.
