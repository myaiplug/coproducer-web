Vertical video is a loudness trap

NoDAW Labs · Lacquer Vol. 02

Shorts, Reels, and Stories normalize differently from music stores, but crushed dialog and clipped music still fail ears. Resize bats do not fix loudness; they fix frames. Measure audio before you batch-resize.

Two pipelines

Social Resize Suite and video FX bats exist to move pixels quickly. Audio legality is a separate drop — analyzer or mix/master bat — unless you enjoy comments about “quiet video.”

Order: fix sound, then batch visuals.

Bulk without regret

Drag-and-drop folder workflows shine when the preset is trusted. Trust comes from measuring one hero clip before you process two hundred.

That habit is cheaper than re-export day.
