Album consistency is a batch problem

NoDAW Labs · Lacquer Vol. 02

Album consistency means every track clears the same delivery gates and sits in a similar loudness lane. That is a batch measurement problem, not a single “final limiter preset.”

Print a lane, not a mood

Measure the album as a set. Note outliers on LUFS, true peak, and clips. Fix outliers before you redesign the whole record.

CoProducer’s batch mindset — and the analyzer .bat that writes HTML/JSON/TXT per bounce — exists so assistants stop hand-copying numbers into spreadsheets.

What “same loudness” does not mean

Ballads can sit slightly quieter than openers without failing the store. They should not clip, and they should not surprise Sound Check with a wild true peak.

Consistency is legal hygiene plus intentional contrast.

Drop a folder on the CoProducer analyzer .bat when you want reports beside every bounce.
Should every track be −14.0 exactly?
Inside the pass window is enough. Exact twins sound unnatural across an album.
