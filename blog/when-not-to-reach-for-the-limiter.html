When not to reach for the limiter

NoDAW Labs · Lacquer Vol. 02

Do not reach for another limiter when clipped samples are already non-zero, when LUFS is only slightly hot, or when the mix problem is arrangement. A limiter can park true peak; it cannot restore flattened transients.

Three stops

If clips exist and you still have the session, fix the clip at the source. If true peak is high and clips are zero, a final true-peak cap is enough. If LUFS is hot and crest is gone, take gain out — more limiting only spends punch the store will not return.

Crazy Stacy’s public pair cleared 831 clips. BeatGoHard needed the ceiling more than a new mix. Different medicine.

Is a limiter always last?
For delivery true-peak control, yes — after loudness moves. In the mix, stop earlier.
Can repair un-clip a vocal?
No. It can stop new overs. Flattened peaks stay flattened.
