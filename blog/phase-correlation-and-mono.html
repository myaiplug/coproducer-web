Phase correlation is a mono audition

NoDAW Labs · Lacquer Vol. 02

Phase correlation near 0 or negative means the mix will thin or cancel in mono. CoProducer treats values under about 0.2 as a fail. Auto-repair will not flip polarity for you — that is mix work.

Why stores still care

Phones, clubs, and some smart speakers collapse channels. A wide chorus that disappears in mono is not “creative stereo.” It is a delivery risk. Correlation is a cheap proxy for that audition.

If correlation fails, check doubled vocals, misaligned layers, and mid/side tricks that emptied the mid. Do not buy a loudness tool to fix a polarity problem.

Order of operations

Fix phase in the session. Then print. Then measure LUFS and true peak. Reversing that order wastes renders.

CoProducer will caution on phase and leave it alone in the automatic plan. That restraint is intentional.

The release checklist keeps phase ≥ 0.2 next to the loudness gates. Read it beside the meter checklist.
Can a stereo widener pass correlation?
Sometimes on the meter, rarely in a real mono fold. Audition mono after every width move.
Is 1.0 required?
No. Fully correlated mono sits near 1.0. Most finished stereo records sit comfortably above 0.2 without being mono.
