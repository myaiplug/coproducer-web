Mastering .bat versus opening the session

NoDAW Labs · Lacquer Vol. 02

A mastering .bat is for trusted delivery passes on finished mixes. Opening the session is for problems the bat cannot see: arrangement, bad edits, clipped vocal comping. Use the bat when the mix is honest.

Honest inputs

If clipped samples are endemic, the session still owns the fix. If the mix is clean and only the ceiling or loudness lane is off, a local mastering FX bat is a time saver.

Before/after demos belong on the site when you supply pairs — until then, trust the analyzer gates first.
