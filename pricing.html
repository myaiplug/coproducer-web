Questions

Before you pay.

Is Pro monthly?

No. $49 once, $34 with WEB30. The old three-tier monthly mock on this URL was a placeholder and did not match the product.

Do I need the web API?

Only to try Analyze in the browser. The desktop app is the real engine: same scoring, plus repair that writes a file.

Will repair restore clipped transients?

No. The report will say so. Limiting can zero the clip count; it cannot un-flatten a peak. Go back to the session when you still can.

What does WEB30 apply to?

CoProducer Pro. Copy it from this page or from a finished Analyze report. It is 30% off $49.
