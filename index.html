    Reference

    Quick answers

    Cloud mastering
    No. CoProducer is local-first analysis and conservative repair. The site can project a repair. The desktop app writes the WAV. Files are not uploaded to a queue.
    Loudness target
    −14 LUFS integrated, within 1.5 LU. True peak at or under −1.0 dBTP. Apple Sound Check is often treated as about −16 LUFS.
    Score of 90
    Release-ready against the gates only: no clipped samples, sample rate at least 44.1 kHz, phase at least 0.2, loudness and true peak in window. It is not a judgment of taste. A missed gate keeps the score under 90.
